Privacy Policy

At Infinity Dimensions S.L. (hereinafter, "Infinity Dimensions", "we") we take seriously the privacy of the people who visit our website and of our clients, candidates and collaborators. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, for what purpose we process it, for how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Data controller

  • Company name: Infinity Dimensions S.L.
  • Registered address: Calle Apolonio Morales 3, 28036 Madrid, Spain
  • Contact email: info@oodimensions.com

2. What data we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process the following categories of data:

  • Contact data: name, company, email, phone number and the content of the message you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Candidate data: CV, cover letter and any information you provide when submitting your application to apply@oodimensions.com.
  • Browsing data: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and time spent, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy).

3. Purpose of processing your data

  • Responding to your inquiries and requests for information about our services.
  • Managing the business relationship with clients and collaborators.
  • Evaluating applications for current or future recruitment processes.
  • Improving the operation and content of our website.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal basis

The processing of your data is based, depending on the case, on: your consent (when filling in a form or sending us your application), the performance of a pre-contractual or contractual relationship, and our legitimate interest in attending to inquiries and improving our services.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and, subsequently, for the legal limitation periods applicable to any resulting liabilities. Applications that are not selected are kept for a maximum of one year, unless you authorize us to keep them for longer.

6. Recipients and disclosures

We do not disclose your data to third parties except where legally required. We may use technology providers (for example, contact form or email delivery services) that act as data processors under contract, with the guarantees required by the GDPR.

7. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability at any time by writing to info@oodimensions.com or by post to Calle Apolonio Morales 3, 28036 Madrid, Spain, attaching a copy of your ID document. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) if you believe we have not processed your data correctly.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss or alteration.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to adapt it to legislative developments or changes in our services. We recommend reviewing it periodically. The date of the last update appears below.
