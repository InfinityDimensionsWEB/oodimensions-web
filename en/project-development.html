Project Development

We digitalize assets and ideas to get the most out of them, supporting our clients from design to the maintenance of their projects.

Modeling

We create 3D models of Architecture, Structures and MEP systems, equipping them with parameters and data. We work with the industry's BIM standards so that every element of the model provides useful information in later phases of the project.

Point Clouds

We build BIM models from point clouds obtained through 3D scanning. A precise starting point that reduces interpretation errors and speeds up the survey of existing buildings and assets.

As-Builts

We generate virtual replicas of physical assets so they can keep producing information throughout their service life. The model becomes the reliable reference for maintenance and future interventions.

Documentation & Management

We coordinate and assist throughout the design, execution and maintenance process of assets. We centralize technical documentation so every project stakeholder works with the same information.

How we work

From scan or drawing to a model with data

We start from whatever stage your project is at —a drawing, a point cloud or an existing asset— and turn it into a BIM model with parameters that are useful for design, construction and maintenance.

  • Data capture through 3D scanning or as-built surveying
  • Modeling with real parameters, not just geometry
  • Delivery and ongoing maintenance of project documentation
