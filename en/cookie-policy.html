Cookie Policy

Welcome to Infinity Dimensions. We want your experience on our website to be as smooth and personalized as possible. That's why we use cookies and similar technologies to improve our services and provide you with relevant content. This Cookie Policy explains how and why we use these technologies on our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. These cookies can be temporary (session cookies) or permanent (persistent cookies). Session cookies are automatically deleted when you close your browser, while persistent cookies remain on your device for a longer period of time.

Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies for several reasons, including:

  • Improving user experience: cookies help us remember your preferences and settings, allowing us to personalize your experience on our website.
  • Analytics and tracking: we use cookies to gather information about how you interact with our website, such as the pages you visit and the time you spend on each one. This helps us improve our services and better understand our users' needs.
  • Personalized advertising: we use cookies to show you ads that are relevant to you based on your interests and browsing behavior.

What types of cookies do we use?

At Infinity Dimensions we use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: these are necessary for the basic operation of our website and allow you to browse it and use its main features.
  • Performance cookies: these collect information about how you use our website, such as the pages you visit and any errors you may encounter. This data helps us improve the performance of our website.
  • Functionality cookies: these allow our website to remember your preferences (such as language or region) and provide you with enhanced, more personalized features.
  • Advertising cookies: these are used to show you ads that are relevant to you and your interests. They may also be used to limit the number of times you see an ad and to measure the effectiveness of an advertising campaign.

How can you manage cookies?

You can manage cookies in your browser by configuring your privacy preferences. Please note that disabling certain cookies may prevent some features and services on our website from working properly.

By continuing to use our website, you accept the use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy of Infinity Dimensions. If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please feel free to contact us at info@oodimensions.com.
