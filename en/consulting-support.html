Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a BIM consultancy engagement actually include?

It depends on the starting point, but it usually covers four things: auditing how the team works today, writing the standards and the BIM execution plan for the project, coordinating models across disciplines so clashes are resolved before they reach site, and training the people who will use it. It is not buying software: it is changing how information is produced and delivered.

How long does it take to implement BIM in a practice or a contractor?

A first pilot project can be running in two or three months. Embedding the methodology across the whole organisation takes longer, usually between six months and a year, because the bottleneck is habit rather than tooling. That is why it is better to start with a real project than with classroom training: people learn when what they model is actually used.

I want to roll out Revit in my company. Where do I start?

By deciding what you need to get out of the model before installing anything: quantities, services coordination, drawing production, or feeding facility management. That answer determines how the template is structured, which families you need and at what level of detail you model. Starting with the licence and learning from tutorials is what produces good-looking models that turn out to be useless.

What does a BIM Manager do, and when do you need one?

They define the rules of the model and make sure they are followed: file structure, naming, levels of detail, delivery workflow and coordination between disciplines. You need one as soon as more than one team touches the same project. With a single modeller you can improvise; with three companies working at once, without someone imposing order the model falls apart in weeks.

What is clash detection and why does it matter?

It means cross-checking the architectural, structural and services models to find where they collide: a duct running through a beam, a drain passing through a column. It matters because solving that on screen costs an afternoon and solving it on site costs money and programme. It is the easiest BIM return on investment to explain to anyone.

Can you work embedded in our team rather than externally?

Yes, we work both ways. As an external team delivering a finished model, or embedded in the client's team using their standards, templates and environment. The second is what usually happens when a methodology already exists and what is missing is capacity.
