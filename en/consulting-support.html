Consulting & Support

We support our clients at every step of BIM adoption: from the first process audit to the daily management of models, so the methodology delivers real value to every project.

Consulting & Support

We advise our clients so their adoption of BIM technology pays off from day one. We analyze each team's starting point and design a realistic, measurable roadmap.

BIM Implementation

We help our clients make the leap to BIM efficiently and intuitively. We define tailored processes, standards and training so the transition doesn't slow down the studio's or contractor's activity.

BIM Management

Model management, documentation creation, workflow definition and quality control. We oversee the full project cycle so information stays consistent across disciplines.

Clash Detection

We coordinate the different disciplines and identify potential interferences before the asset is built. Detecting conflicts in the model prevents cost overruns and delays on site.

Information Management

We analyze asset data and prepare it to create reports that support decision-making. We organize information so it's available when the project needs it.

Clash detection

Conflicts get solved in the model, not on site

When a duct runs through a beam in the model, fixing it costs a click. When it shows up on site, it costs days and money. We cross-check disciplines —architecture, structure and MEP— so every interference is detected, assigned and resolved before construction.

  • Automatic detection of interferences between disciplines
  • Prioritized, traceable clash reports
  • Follow-up until every conflict is resolved
